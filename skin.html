Collagen & Your Skin

Discover the fascinating protein that helps give your skin strength, structure and elasticity.

What Is Collagen?

🧬 A Protein

Collagen is the most abundant protein in the human body. It acts as an important structural component of many tissues.

🏗️ Structural Support

It helps provide strength and structure to the skin, bones, tendons, ligaments and cartilage.

✨ Important for Skin

A large amount of the skin's structural framework is made from collagen, particularly in the dermis.

How Is Collagen Related to Skin?

The skin has several layers. Collagen is especially important in the dermis, the layer beneath the outer epidermis.

Collagen fibers form part of the skin's structural network. Together with other proteins such as elastin, they help the skin maintain its structure and resilience.

What Happens as We Age?

⏳ Collagen Production

As people age, the body's production of collagen generally decreases.

〰️ Skin Changes

Changes in collagen and other structural components can contribute to reduced skin firmness and elasticity.

☀️ Environmental Factors

Excessive ultraviolet (UV) exposure can damage collagen and contribute to premature skin aging.

How Can You Support Healthy Skin?

  • Eat a balanced diet containing adequate protein.
  • Eat foods rich in vitamin C, which is needed for collagen synthesis.
  • Protect your skin from excessive UV exposure.
  • Avoid smoking, which can damage collagen and accelerate skin aging.
  • Maintain good overall health and hydration.

The Big Picture

Collagen is like part of the skin's internal scaffolding.

It doesn't simply "make your skin beautiful." Instead, collagen provides structural support that helps the skin maintain its strength and resilience.

As collagen production and organization change with age and environmental exposure, visible changes such as reduced firmness and the development of wrinkles can occur.
