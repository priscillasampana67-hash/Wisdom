It's Been a While

A story about time, memories, and second chances

“It’s been a while.”

Those were the first words she said when she finally saw him again.

She had imagined that moment so many times. In her imagination, she would run toward him, hug him tightly, and tell him how much she had missed him. She had even rehearsed what she would say.

But when he actually stood in front of her, she could only smile.

“It’s been a while,” she repeated.

He smiled back. “Yeah. It has.”

They stood there quietly, surrounded by people rushing past them. Cars moved along the road, music played somewhere in the distance, and the evening breeze gently moved her hair.

But for a moment, none of that mattered.

All she could see was him.

It had been almost two years since they had last spoken properly. Two years of birthdays missed, messages never sent, memories revisited, and countless nights wondering how different things might have been if they had simply tried a little harder.

Life had taken them in different directions.

She had gone to university. He had started working. They made new friends, learned new things, and became different versions of the people they used to be.

Yet somehow, standing there together again, it felt as though no time had passed.

“You look different,” he said.

She laughed. “Different good or different bad?”

“Different good.”

She looked down, trying to hide her smile.

“And you?” she asked. “You haven't changed.”

He smiled. “That's a lie.”

“No. You still have that same smile.”

He looked at her for a moment.

“And you still remember.”

She didn't answer.

How could she explain that she remembered everything?

The little arguments.

The stupid jokes.

The late-night conversations.

The promises they made when they thought the future belonged entirely to them.

She remembered the day they stopped talking, too.

Neither of them had really wanted to say goodbye. They had simply allowed silence to grow between them until it became too big to cross.

“Do you ever think about what happened?” he asked.

She took a deep breath.

“Sometimes.”

“Sometimes?”

She smiled sadly.

“Okay. A lot.”

He nodded.

“Me too.”

Another silence followed.

But this silence was different.

It wasn't uncomfortable.

It was filled with everything they had never said.

Finally, he asked, “Do you think we could start again?”

She looked at him.

Not the boy she remembered.

Not the person she had spent two years missing.

But the person standing in front of her now.

Maybe that was the point.

People change.

Time changes people.

And sometimes, when you meet someone again after a long time, you don't get the old version of them back.

You get a chance to know who they've become.

She smiled.

“Maybe.”

He smiled too.

“Maybe is better than nothing.”

They began walking together, slowly, talking about everything and nothing.

And as the sun disappeared beyond the horizon, she realized something.

Sometimes, “it’s been a while” isn't the end of a story.

Sometimes, it's the beginning of another chapter.
