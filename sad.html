When Discrete Maths Broke My Heart 😭

It was finally over. The Discrete Mathematics examination that I had been preparing for was finished, but instead of feeling relieved, I just sat quietly at my desk, staring into space.

I had tried. I had studied modular arithmetic, number theory, counting, graphs, Big O notation, theorems and so many other topics. I had solved questions until my head hurt. I honestly thought I was ready.

But when I entered the examination hall, everything suddenly felt different. Some of the questions looked familiar, but my mind struggled to remember what to do. I looked at the clock, then back at the questions. The minutes seemed to disappear so quickly.

When the examination ended, I walked out of the hall with a heavy heart. I kept thinking about the questions I could not solve and the mistakes I might have made.

The Moment I Felt Like Giving Up

I got home, put my bag down and sat on my bed. I felt sad. I kept asking myself, "Did all that studying really matter?"

For a moment, I felt like I had disappointed myself. I wanted to do well. I wanted to prove that I could handle Computer Science. Discrete Mathematics was difficult, and the examination had reminded me just how challenging this journey could be.

But after sitting there for a while, I remembered something important: one examination does not define me.

I may not have performed the way I wanted to. I may have made mistakes. I may even be disappointed when the results come out. But I am still learning.

Tomorrow Is Another Day 🌱

Maybe this exam did not go the way I hoped, but my story does not end in that examination hall. I still have more things to learn, more problems to solve and more opportunities to improve.

So tonight, I will allow myself to be sad. I will take a deep breath, rest my mind and remind myself:

"I did my best with what I knew. I can learn from this, and I will come back stronger."

Discrete Mathematics may have broken my heart today... but it will not break my dreams. ❤️
