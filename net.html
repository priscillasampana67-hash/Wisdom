Computer Networking

Introduction

Computer networking is the process of connecting two or more computers and other devices so they can communicate and share resources such as files, printers, and internet access.

Types of Networks

  • LAN (Local Area Network)
  • MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)
  • WAN (Wide Area Network)

Networking Devices

  1. Router
  2. Switch
  3. Hub
  4. Bridge
  5. Repeater
  6. Modem

Advantages of Networking

  • Sharing files and information
  • Sharing hardware resources
  • Easy communication
  • Internet sharing
  • Remote access to data

Conclusion

Computer networking is an important part of modern technology. It helps individuals and organizations communicate efficiently, share resources, and access information quickly.
